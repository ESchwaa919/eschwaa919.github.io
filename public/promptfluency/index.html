The CRISP Framework

Our battle-tested framework for crafting effective prompts that drive results

📋 Context

Set the stage with relevant background information. The more context you provide, the more tailored and accurate the response.

🎭 Role

Define the AI's perspective. Asking it to act as an expert in a specific domain can dramatically improve response quality.

📝 Instructions

Be clear and specific about what you want. Break complex requests into step-by-step instructions.

📊 Structure

Specify the desired format of the output—bullets, tables, code, or narrative prose.

💡 Prompting Techniques

Leverage advanced techniques like chain-of-thought reasoning, few-shot examples, and self-consistency.

Essential Best Practices

✓

Be Specific: Vague prompts yield vague results. Clear, detailed instructions produce superior outputs.

✓

Iterate & Refine: Treat prompting as a conversation. Use follow-up questions to guide the AI toward better results.

✓

Provide Examples: Show the AI what you want. Few-shot learning dramatically improves output quality.

✓

Set Constraints: Define boundaries—word count, tone, format, or style—to keep outputs focused.

✓

Use Chain-of-Thought: Ask the AI to show its reasoning process for complex problem-solving tasks.

✓

Protect Sensitive Data: Never share confidential information. Anonymize data before processing.
