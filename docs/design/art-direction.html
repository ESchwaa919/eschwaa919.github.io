§01 — Palette

One light source.
Everything else is night.

The palette is a cinema at night: a cold near-black room, warm off-white text, and a single emerald signal. Emerald is earned — it marks the narrative thread, live data, and the primary action. If a section uses it more than twice, it's using it wrong. Magenta, cyan and purple are retired.

§03 — Structural devices

The scene marker is the signature.

Every act opens with a screenplay slate: a mono label naming the scene, and a hairline rule that draws in from the left as it enters the viewport. It encodes the true sequence of the story — the homepage genuinely is acts, the process genuinely is stages. Never use it on content that isn't sequential. Corner brackets, glitch effects and scan-lines are retired.

§05 — Motion

Nothing moves unless the story does.

The motion vocabulary (all of it)

  • Reveal — opacity 0→1, translateY 18px→0, 0.7s, cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1), stagger 80ms. Via useScrollReveal.
  • Rule draw — scene-marker hairline scaleX 0→1, 1.2s, on enter.
  • Hover — cards lift 3px + border warms; buttons lift 2px + single soft glow.
  • Scroll cue — slow 3s opacity pulse. The only looping animation on the site.
  • Reduced motion — everything above collapses to static. Non-negotiable.

Retired permanently

  • text-shimmer / gradient-animate headlines
  • glitch, scan-line, data-stream grid pan
  • floating orbs, animate-float badges
  • pulse-glow icons, border-glow loops
  • btn-shimmer sweep
  • hover scale-105 (scale reads as toy; lift reads as cinema)
§06 — Continuity: keep / retire

Kept from cyber-luxe

  • Near-black canvas — now colder: hsl(200 25% 4%)
  • Emerald identity — same hue, hsl(155 100% 45%), now solo
  • Grain overlay — at 0.035 opacity
  • Dark glass nav — blur(12px) on the header only
  • Inter for body copy
  • useScrollReveal animation infrastructure

Retired

  • Magenta/pink, cyan, purple accents + all their glows
  • Rajdhani display face
  • Cyber-grid hero image, floating orbs, corner brackets
  • Glassmorphism on cards (flat surface + hairline instead)
  • Neon text-shadows on headings (glow lives in ONE place: the hero horizon)
  • Erik headshot as the homepage hero
